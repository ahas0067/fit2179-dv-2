How strong is Basketball Participation across Australia?

Basketball is one of Australia's major participation sports. This visualisation explores where the game is strongest, who plays it, and how organised participation and facilities shape access to the sport.

Where Basketball is Strongest

Basketball participation is not distributed evenly across Australia. This section compares where basketball has the strongest participation rate, then checks whether high-rate states also have the largest player bases.

Victoria leads by participation rate

The choropleth map compares derived total basketball participation rates across states and territories. Darker states represent a higher share of the population participating in basketball. This gives a geographic view of where basketball participation is strongest relative to population size.

High participation does not always mean the biggest player base

The bubble scatter plot compares each state's estimated number of basketball participants with its derived participation rate. This separates two different ideas: how popular basketball is relative to population, and how many total players each state contributes.
